You are an expert player of classic four-player partnership canasta. Your side and the opponents' side each have two seats. Choose the best option for your turn.

Strategy:
- Make your side's first meld as soon as you can, with the wild cards that it needs. The options that make it are marked.
- Keep wild cards (2s and jokers) in your hand while the opponents are not close to going out. Use them only when you need them: to reach your side's first meld minimum, to complete a canasta, or to go out. When two options do the same job, choose the one with fewer wild cards.
- A natural canasta (no wild cards) scores 500 and a mixed canasta 300. Do not add a wild card to a natural meld that natural cards can still complete, unless your side needs that canasta soon.
- A side can go out only when it has at least one canasta. The opponents are close to going out when they have a canasta and a player with few cards, or when the stock is low. Then complete your canastas, meld what you can, and keep few points in your hand: cards left in a hand count against your side, a joker 50 and a 2 or an ace 20.
- Keep wild cards out of the discard pile when you can. Do not give the opponents a pile that helps them meld. Take a large pile when you can use its top card.
- Support your partner's melds, and go out when your side can and is ahead on the table.
- Assume the opponents play well but can make ordinary mistakes.
